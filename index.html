How it works

1

Enrol the patient

Add the patient at discharge with the follow-up date, medicines and advice. The patient agrees to check-ins, and you record that.

2

Send one link

Tap to share the patient's private check-in link on WhatsApp. They answer in English, Hindi or Marathi. No app, no login.

3

See who to call

Red, amber or green for every patient, with a clear list of who needs an early call, who wants another date and who has not confirmed.

Know when to call each patient back

Your follow-up list sorts itself every time a patient checks in.

Built for patient privacy

Only you see your patients

Each doctor's patients are visible only to that doctor. This is enforced by the database itself.

Consent first

You cannot enrol a patient without confirming they agreed to check-ins. Patients can stop check-ins from their own link at any time.

Verified doctors

We check your medical council registration before you can add patients.
