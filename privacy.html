Privacy and patient data

YORO Recover is operated by CuroClan Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (YORO Health), Pune. It helps doctors follow up with patients after discharge.

What we collect

  • Doctors: name, clinic, specialty, city, mobile number and, if given, medical council registration number.
  • Patients (entered by their doctor): name, mobile number, procedure, discharge and follow-up dates, and any advice, medicines or tests the doctor adds.
  • Patient check-ins: answers about pain, medicines, symptoms, fever and the follow-up visit, plus any note the patient writes.

Why

Only to let the treating doctor see how a patient is recovering and decide when to call them. We do not sell data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the patient's own doctor (and their clinic account). Database rules prevent one doctor from seeing another doctor's patients. A small number of YORO staff may access records for support and security, under confidentiality.

Consent and your choices

  • A patient's doctor confirms the patient agreed to check-ins before enrolling them.
  • A patient can stop check-ins at any time using the link at the bottom of the check-in page.
  • A doctor can permanently delete a patient and all their check-ins from the portal. To ask us to delete or correct data, write to care@yorohealth.com.

Security

Data is stored in an encrypted database and sent over HTTPS. Each patient's check-in link contains a private random code; anyone with the link can answer that patient's check-in, so patients should not share it.

Not for emergencies

YORO Recover is a follow-up tool. It does not diagnose and is not monitored around the clock. In an emergency call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.
